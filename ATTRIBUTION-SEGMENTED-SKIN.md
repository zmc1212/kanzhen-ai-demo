# Third-party 3D skin model attribution

This demo includes `models/skin-segmented.glb`, a 256-part segmented skin-surface model from:

- Project: [3d-human-body-anatomy](https://github.com/SeemaBansal1/3d-human-body-anatomy)
- Upstream asset: `web/public/models/skin.glb`
- Upstream asset SHA-1: `1000d249050112ea014651647d3145e0bde127f8`
- Source: Z-Anatomy (libre 3D atlas of anatomy), built on BodyParts3D.
- Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)** for the Z-Anatomy-derived model data. The source repository states its derived model layers are simplified and modified for the web and are shared under the same licence.

Required attribution:
- **Z-Anatomy – The libre 3D atlas of anatomy – CC BY-SA 4.0**
- **BodyParts3D – The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS) – CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan**

Upstream source licence and attribution details:
- [Upstream source licence](https://github.com/SeemaBansal1/3d-human-body-anatomy/blob/main/source/License.txt)
- [Upstream model pipeline and credits](https://github.com/SeemaBansal1/3d-human-body-anatomy#credits-and-licences)

The asset is redistributed unchanged in this demo. The application loads its individual named mesh parts and applies runtime selection highlighting; no claim of ownership is made over the model geometry. Any redistributed adaptations of this Z-Anatomy-derived model must comply with CC BY-SA 4.0, including attribution and ShareAlike requirements.

The upstream project warns that some non-skin assets (including a kidney in its organs layer) have separate non-commercial terms. This demo uses only the segmented skin layer from that project, not its organs layer.
